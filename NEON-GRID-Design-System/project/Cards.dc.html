CARD RULES
Whole card is one link. Wrap it in a single <a> so it gets one tab stop and one focus ring. Tilt: max ±6° on Y, ±4° on X, lift 4px, motion/base cyber-out. In product, tilt follows the pointer; reduced motion disables tilt and keeps the border glow. Accent per type: district = cyan, character = magenta brackets, augment = cyan glow + yellow NEW tag. Locked / disabled: 50% opacity, desaturated art, a LOCKED chip and aria-disabled. Never hide locked content entirely. Image placeholders in brackets are where 3D renders go; keep 16:10 for districts, 4:5 for characters.
