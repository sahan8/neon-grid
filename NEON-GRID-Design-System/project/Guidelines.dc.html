07 / GUIDELINES ACCESSIBILITY · DO / DON'T · TOKENS

Accessibility

CONTRAST · AA 17.8:1 text/primary on bg/void. text/muted is 6.6:1. Every text pairing in this system passes 4.5:1; neon/violet (3.5:1) is decoration only.
FOCUS RING
FOCUSED
outline 2px #00F0FF, offset 3px + glow/cyan. Use :focus-visible. On clipped elements, ring the wrapper.
TAP TARGET
44
Minimum 44×44px hit area for every control, including icon buttons, chips that act, footer links on mobile.
MOTION + FLASH ≤ 3 / s Glitch never flashes more than 3 times a second. prefers-reduced-motion swaps every animation for a fade; also offer an in-page "Reduce FX" toggle.
Overlays stay out of the way. Scanlines and grain are pointer-events: none, at 4% and 3%, and sit above backgrounds but never change text contrast measurably. 3D has a fallback. Every WebGL scene has a static poster image, a text alternative, and pauses when off-screen or when the tab is hidden. Uppercase is short. Uppercase only for display headings, buttons and mono labels; body copy and anything over ~6 words stays sentence case.
